Founded in Costa Rica in **2025**, tria is a software engineering company that builds products for clients across **North America, Europe, and LATAM**. From high-converting landing pages to enterprise platforms, we design, build, and scale technology that drives measurable business outcomes.

We work across the full software spectrum — product design, cloud architecture, full-stack development, data, AI, and security — and operate as a **remote-first company rooted in Costa Rica**.
